## Why V16 loses to 2600+ teams (exact replays of V16's 29 games against 2500+)

* Labour is not the gap. From day 8 both sides spend about 6,000 unit-turns,
  about 42% of them walking, and take the same CARE/FEED/WATER counts.
* **The herd is chosen by the first shop only.** When yarn stores appear as the
  2nd-4th shop, V16 keeps its cow plan (7-10 cows, 5-11 sheep). Strong opponents
  move to 12 sheep by day 10 and 14-18 by day 12, with the same ~11 hands.
  Examples:

  | Episode | Opponent | Margin | Yarn stores | Sheep (us / them) | Wool (us / them) |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 114250254 | 2616 | −46k | 2 | 5 / 15 | $22k / $84k |
  | 114121565 | 2646 | −32k | 4 | 11 / 18 | $47k / $87k |

  When the first shop is a yarn store, V16 runs 19-20 sheep and wins (+$5k, +$16k).
* **Tomatoes:** 2600+ opponents sell $6.7k of tomatoes a game; V16 sells $0.9k.
* The wheat revenue gap is buy-and-resell: they buy $48k of wheat to resell.
* **CARE is the most valuable action in the engine.** A fed and cared-for day adds
  +1 unit at the next production, so a sheep yields about 1.33 wool a day rather
  than 0.33.
* A herd swap keeps the set of empty tiles unchanged. The weed/shop random draws
  stay aligned, so replaying V16's real games stays valid for this change.

1. **Base: frontier V17** (ChatGPT line, submission 56605749). It is the strongest agent
   we have, measured head-to-head on 16 seeds with seat 0 only:

   | Agent | vs v14 | vs v16 | vs haideptry (2965) |

   On the live ladder (2026-09-27 ~14:00 UTC), V17 was 41-8 over 49 games with a
   +$11.1k mean margin. Its rating was still climbing: 1348 after 49 games.
2. **Frontier V18 land retry.** This is ChatGPT's `frontier_v18_land_retry_unconditional`:
   on days 9-14, if the farm still owns 2 quadrants, it retries the third purchase,
   keeping $3,000 in reserve.
3. **Land-debt recovery** (new, Claude line):
   * **Why:** a route's `BUY_LAND` silently no-ops when cash is short. In V17's three
     catastrophic ladder losses (−$76k to −$138k), the day-6 north-east purchase
     failed with $29-400 in the bank. It went through on day 8, and the south-west
     purchase then came days late. The rival was at 3 quadrants while we were at 2.
   * **What:** the layer remembers every quadrant the parent tried to buy. From
     day 6 to day 16, while we own fewer, it re-issues `BUY_LAND` on the first turn
     that cash covers the parent's own orders plus the land price.
   * **Capped at 3 quadrants** (`LD_MAX_Q = 3`). The V17 routes also ask for the
     south-east quadrant on day 8 and never afford it. Chasing that $4,000
     quadrant as well (the uncapped variant) changed every game and cost
     **−$3,660 a game**.
