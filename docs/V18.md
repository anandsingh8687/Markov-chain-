   On the live ladder (2026-09-27 ~14:00 UTC), V17 was 41-8 over 49 games with a
   +$11.1k mean margin. Its rating was still climbing: 1348 after 49 games.
2. **Frontier V18 land retry.** This is ChatGPT's `frontier_v18_land_retry_unconditional`:
   on days 9-14, if the farm still owns 2 quadrants, it retries the third purchase,
   keeping $3,000 in reserve.
3. **Land-debt recovery** (new, Claude line):
   * **Why:** a route's `BUY_LAND` silently no-ops when cash is short. In V17's three
     catastrophic ladder losses (−$76k to −$138k), the day-6 north-east purchase
     failed with $29-400 in the bank. It went through on day 8, and the south-west
     purchase then came days late. The rival was at 3 quadrants while we were at 2.
   * **What:** the layer remembers every quadrant the parent tried to buy. From
     day 6 to day 16, while we own fewer, it re-issues `BUY_LAND` on the first turn
     that cash covers the parent's own orders plus the land price.
   * **Capped at 3 quadrants** (`LD_MAX_Q = 3`). The V17 routes also ask for the
     south-east quadrant on day 8 and never afford it. Chasing that $4,000
     quadrant as well (the uncapped variant) changed every game and cost
     **−$3,660 a game**.
