# v9: fix the cash crunch that lost v8's two biggest games

## v8 on the ladder

v8 (submission 56503612) went live on 2026-09-23. Over its first 94 ladder games
it went **79-15 (84%)** with no ties, and was rated **2188.9** on 2026-09-24 while
still climbing. Over the same window v7 went 9-6-25.

All 15 losses were downloaded and replayed. The local engine reproduces every
one of them to the dollar. They fall into two groups:

| Group | Games | Size | What happened |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Close | 13 | −$20 to −$1,858 | The opponent runs a near-clone of the same route and wins on sale timing, or grows carrot where we grow wheat |
| Collapse | 2 | **−$28,063**, −$10,813 | See below |

## The collapse

Both big losses follow the same chain:

1. **Turn 0.** The opening's wheat buy-and-sell runs in per-unit lockstep against
   a rival with a slightly different opening. It comes out a few dollars dearer
   for us than usual.
2. **End of day 0.** The tape spends the day's cash down to the dollar, so we end
   the day with **$1**. The rival ends it with $22.
3. **Day 1, hour 0.** Hands are wiped every night and re-hired, and the n-th hire
   of a day costs fib(n): $1, $1, $2, $3, and so on. The tape hires 3 hands for $4.
   We can afford 1.
4. **Day 2.** Two hands short for a day means less watering and feeding, which
   means less income, so the day-2 cow purchase fails.
5. **The rest of the game.** Every later animal purchase is a turn late or fails.
   We finish with 5 sheep and 5 cows against the rival's 9 and 6, and half its
   wool.

The replay confirms it. Across the 45 v8 ladder games checked, a hire failed in
exactly two, and those are these two losses.

## The fix

The **morning-hire reserve** is appended to `main.py`. From hour 12 of each day it
reads how many hands the tape hires at the next hour 0 and computes their cost.
If this turn's orders would leave less than that, it trims seed purchases,
starting from the end of the list, until the cost is covered. Seeds are the
cheapest thing on the list to lose: one missing wheat seed costs one plant,
while two missing hands cost a day of the whole schedule.

The guard does nothing unless cash would actually run short. In the two collapse
games it removed a single $10 wheat seed:

## Tried for v9 and rejected

Each was measured head-to-head against v8 on 16 fresh seeds, and on v8's
downloaded ladder games:

* **Carrot swap at a lower price ratio** (1.5 instead of 1.8, and with the last
  day raised from 23 to 25): 0 W, 13 T, 3 L against v8. Close losses to carrot
  growers are not fixed by growing more carrot.
* **Race horizon 46:** 5-6-5 against v8 (−$34).
* **Race horizon 42:** 1-2-13 against v8.
* **Counter D budget 2,000 instead of 800:** 3-13-0 (+$3). Too small to be worth
  the extra time per turn.
* **Alternative routes for yarn-shop layouts:** the table's route 9 won 76 of 90
  ghost games (+$2,716). Route 0 won 57 (+$906), and routes 11, 12 and 128 were
  far worse.
