## The breakthrough

The competition overview says both players sell into one market, unit by unit, alternating.
So an opponent's orders move the prices a fixed route plan was tuned on.

V16's day-0 plan buys wheat seed ($10 each) until its cash runs out:

* against Pipe-7 it ends day 0 with $7, and the day-1 hires ($4) succeed;
* against Fieldbook, the opponent's day-0 orders shift prices by $3. V16 buys one more seed,
  ends day 0 with **$0**, and every day-1 HIRE fails. The plan then collapses: −$20k to
  −$77k.

The strongest public agents (Fieldbook, v58 "Known Streams", boatlee v13, kaito v21) beat
V16/V17/V18d this way.

**FUNDGUARD (surgical)**, applied each turn:

* From hour 12, keep enough cash for the next morning's first 3 hires ($4 + $3 margin).
  Drop a seed or fertilizer buy that would spend it.
* If a HIRE, land, animal or seed order would still fail for lack of cash, sell shed
  stock first.

**Rejected: a blunt variant** that reserved the cost of all of today's hires. It fixed the
exploit but lost 10 wins against mid-tier agents (48 → 38 in league 2).
