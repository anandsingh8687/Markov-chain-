# v11: win the late-game sale races against our own lineage

v11 is v10 with one change to the base's lead-sellers. From day 18 (turn 432) on,
they look 12 turns ahead of the route tape instead of 8. Strawberries keep the
8-turn look-ahead. The lead-sellers are the dawn, midday and evening windows
that pull planned sales forward.

## Why

At v10's rating (rank ~332, 2495.6 on 2026-09-25), most losses are close races
against agents running the same route tape. All 111 of v10's ladder games were
downloaded and replayed; v10 reproduces 110 of them to the dollar. In the 29
close losses (under $2,000), both banks match **to the dollar until about day
15-24** and only diverge in the last week.

One example, episode 113169060, lost by $1,157. The opponent sold the same
wool and milk as we did, but an hour earlier: 11 wool at hour 9 for $181-154,
against ours at hour 10 for $151-112, and 9 milk at hour 9 while we sold in lots
of 3 from hour 12. The first seller takes the top of the price curve.

### Tomato swap (Roxy's method): built, tested, rejected for now

v12a swaps the tape's day-18 wheat replants for tomatoes in towns with exactly
two tomato buyers. The tape keeps watering the swapped tiles, and a crew hired
on days 25-29 fertilizes and harvests them. On the 5 qualifying seeds, against
prv_rain and the public base (10 games): **−$2,204 a game against v11's
+$1,170, 1 win out of 10.**

* Only 7 of the tape's day-18 replants can be swapped, and they are scattered
  along the farm's edges, 7-9 steps from the shed. The crew spends most of its
  day walking.
* Yield was 4.4 tomatoes a plant, against Roxy's 7.4 and a possible 8.
* The tape's wheat on those tiles is worth about $550 a tile over 11 days. That
  is more than the tomatoes earned after about $2k of crew wages.

Other lessons:

* Planting on day 17 is too early: those plants rot on day 29 holding 4 units.
  Day 19 is too late: the last yield cannot be sold.
* The tape's own fertilizer layer empties the shed every morning, so a crew
  must buy its own fertilizer.
* Other layers also hire mid-morning, so the crew must take the next free hand
  indices instead of assuming the tape's count.
