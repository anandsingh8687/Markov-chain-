# v14 candidate: trade young strawberries for tomatoes in strawberry-poor towns (STX)

Status: **candidate, not submitted** (`candidates/v14/main.py`). The live agent is
v13 (`main.py`, submission 56581155).

v14 is v13 plus one layer, STX (`research/v14/stx_layer.py`, spliced in by
`build_stx.py`). TOMATO is also added to MDX's 5am/5pm sale.

## 1. Why: the games above 2500 are mirror matches

`research/tools/v12/routeid.py` identifies each side's route from its daily seed
and animal purchases. Nearly every opponent from 2000 to 2650 runs our route tape
**and picks the same route** (the same shop table). Two ideas were rejected:

* Switching to the other route built for the same town. Across the 163 games
  against 2450+ opponents (`eps_hi.txt`), it wins 81 (r1) and 109 (r2) against
  v13's 121. Keep the router.
* Tomatoes in the tape's wheat rows (TMS, `research/v14/tms_layer.py`). Wheat is
  the herd's feed: sheep escaped, and it wins 33/72 against 59. Buying the feed
  back does not fix it (31/72). With a wheat reserve required, it never fires.

What the strongest tape copies add is tomatoes. In 9 of v13's 31 replay losses
to 2500+ opponents, the opponent sold 40-120 tomatoes and we sold none
(`oppprof.py`). `farmmap.py` shows how chocolat (2621) does it:
* on day 14 it hires two extra hands;
* they DIG 8 young strawberry plants in the SW cluster next to the shed and
  PLANT TOMATO there;
* the tape keeps watering, fertilizing and harvesting "its strawberries" on
  those tiles, so the tomatoes are looked after for free.

Engine: a tomato first fruits at age 8, then +1 a day (+2 when fertilized) for
four days, holding up to 4 on the plant.

## 2. STX

On day 14, once the tape's own hires are done:
* it buys K tomato seeds and hires 2 hands;
* they DIG the K youngest strawberries (age 0-5) within 5 steps of the shed and
  PLANT TOMATO;
* afterwards, a tape unit whose order on a tomato tile would be a no-op waters
  it, or harvests it once it holds fruit;
* fruit reaches the shed at midnight and MDX sells it.

V219 (3+ tomato buyers on day 18) is told to ignore STX's tomatoes.

**The gate matters.** Ungated (x1), STX wins 50/72 against v13's 59. By town on
day 14 (`feat14.py`):

| Strawberry buyers | Tomato buyers ≥ 1: games | Mean vs v13 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-1 | 14 | +$2,200 (11 better) |
| 2 | 21 | −$2,300 |
| 3-4 | 9 | −$8,000 |

With two or more strawberry buyers (brunch, ice cream, smoothie, farmers
market), the late strawberry quote holds up, so the dug plants were worth more
than the tomatoes. With 0-1 it crashes to $5-25, and tomatoes at $60-80 win.
The gate was learned on the 72 games, so the three sets below are the
independent test.
