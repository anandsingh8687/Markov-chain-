# v10: the strongest public base, plus v9's layers

Submitted 2026-09-25 as Kaggle submission 56540053 (from `main.py` at f7d5571).
At submission time v9 (56525155) was rated 2291.0.

v10 (`main.py`) is three pieces:

* **Base:** tetsutani's "Demand-Preserving Turn Sale Timing" (public Kaggle
  notebook, Apache-2.0, released 2026-09-24; also republished byte-identical as
  cha22, Master Engine V3 and Multi-Route). It is the same route-tape lineage as
  v9, plus newer selling and ordering layers: WHEAT BUY-FIRST, the FLOWPX, DAWNPX
  and MODELPX lead-sellers, BUYDIP, SHIELD-MILK, a fertilizer guard, a late seed
  cap, same-item SELL compaction and queue hole-closure. Its upstream notices are
  kept at the top of the file.
* **v9's level-2 counter D.** The base orders its market list with level-1
  counter D, which is a best response to a rival that plays the plain list. v10
  replaces it with v9's level-2 search, which chooses an order that is safe both
  against that rival and against a rival that is itself running level 1. That
  rival is exactly a copy of the public base.
* **v9's morning-hire reserve** (see `docs/V9.md`), kept as a safety net.
